\section{Olympus ensemble}
\label{sec:results-olympus}

\Cref{fig:olympus-summaries} summarizes the findings of the Olympus ensemble study case 
by averaging posterior observation coverages and mean square errors across the 10 ground-truths selected.
The FOPT CDF suggests that ground-truths are somewhat biased towards extreme values.
Standard DSI-ESMDA is indicated by gating intensity 0.
For more details on this case study's results, see
\cref{sec:detailed-olympus-ensemble}.
%
OC and MSE clearly tended to increase with gating intensity,
albeit with much larger deviations than the Egg case. 
MSE, in particular, remained well beyond the okay limit, 
and in worst cases did not even satisfy the ``fair'' limit.
%
Also contrasting with the Egg case, localization was shown to significantly 
improve OC and, to a lesser extent, reduce MSE.
