\chapter{Detailed results}
\label{chap:detailed-results}

This appendix expounds the results shown in \cref{chap:results}
by presenting tables and figures whose placement within the main body of the manuscript would have been unwieldy.

For the synthetic 
Egg (\cref{sec:detailed-egg-ensemble}) and 
Olympus (\cref{sec:detailed-olympus-ensemble})
ensembles, the observation coverage (OC), mean squared error (MSE), and data mismatch 
of the posterior distributions are tabled for each experiment conducted.

Since the OC, MSE and mismatch of the real field have already been presented in the main body of the manuscript,
they are not repeated here. 
%
Instead, \cref{sec:detailed-real_field-ensemble} 
presents the plots of the posterior ensemble generated by running the proposed Gated DSI-ESMDA algorithm
with a gating intensity of $\lambda=4$.
%
As \cref{tab:real-dsi-esmda,tab:real-gated-dsi-esmda-1-16} indicate, 
this level of gating produces results similar to those using the standard DSI-ESMDA,
albeit with a slightly better coverage in exchange of a slightly increased mismatch.
