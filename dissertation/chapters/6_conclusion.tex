\debug{%
The job of the conclusion is to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Fully and clearly articulate the answer to your research questions
    \item Discuss how the research is related to your aims and objectives
    \item Explain the significance of the work
    \item Outline its shortcomings
    \item Suggest avenues for future research
\end{itemize}
}

\debug{%
It is not the place to introduce new ideas and concepts, or to present new findings. 
Your job is to reflect back on your original aims and intentions and discuss them in terms of your findings and new expertise.
}

\debug{%
By the time the reader has finished reading the conclusion, they should be able to answer the following questions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Have you briefly recapped the research questions and objectives?
    \item Have you provided a brief recount of the answer to those questions?
    \item Have you clearly discussed the significance and implications of those findings?
    \item Have you discussed the contribution that the study has made?
    \item Do the claims you are making align with the content of the results and discussion chapters?
\end{itemize}
}
