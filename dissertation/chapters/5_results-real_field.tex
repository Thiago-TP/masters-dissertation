\section{Brazilian post-salt field}
\label{sec:results-real-field}

For this case study analysis, only the real field was picked as ground-truth 
for running the experiments,
so results need not be averaged/summarized.
%
\Cref{sec:detailed-real_field-ensemble} plots the posterior ensemble from Gated DSI-ESMDA with $\lambda=4$.
%
\Cref{fig:fopt-real} shows that real data
nearly squarely at the 50th percentile of FOPT.
%
Main findings for the standard DSI-ESMDA are presented in \cref{tab:real-dsi-esmda},
while results for the proposed Gated DSI-ESMDA for varying gating are shown in 
\cref{tab:real-gated-dsi-esmda-1-16}.
%
As in the Egg case,
localization did not have a significant impact on observation coverages or data mismatches,
and both OC and MSE tended to grow with gating intensity.
%
MSE of the posteriors tended not to be much lower than the priors.
