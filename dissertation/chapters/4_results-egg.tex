\section{Egg ensemble}
\label{sec:results-egg}

\Cref{fig:egg-summaries} summarizes the findings of the Egg ensemble study case 
by averaging posterior observation coverages and mean square errors across the 10 ground-truths selected.
The FOPT CDF suggests that ground-truths are fairly unbiased.
Standard DSI-ESMDA is indicated by gating intensity 0.
For more details on this case study's results, see
\cref{sec:detailed-egg-ensemble}.
%
OC and MSE clearly tended to increase with gating intensity,
and with relatively small standard deviations. 
MSE, in particular, remained fairly close a threshold deemed ``okay'' in reservoir prediction literature
\cite[subsection 9.4.2]{Emerick:2025}.
%
OC and MSE were found insensitive to localization (green bars) for intense enough gating.
