    capping its discussion with a commentary on the cumulative distributions of field oil production totals.
    \Cref{sec:evaluation-workflow-parameters} presents the workflow designed to generate and evaluate results on each case study,
    including what metrics were used, 
    how experiment parameters were set, and what data features were used.

\chaptersummary{%
    This chapter has presented the necessary tools for understanding how this works findings were obtained.
    From the CDFs of FOPTs, it was seen that Egg realizations are very simple, while Olympus is closer in complexity to the real field.
    Posterior distributions of reservoir predictions are evaluated across four metrics,
    observation coverage (of the history and validation periods),
    mean square error (history period only), and 
    data mismatch (history period only).
    The general workflow of an experiment consists of loading data, normalizing it, 
    constructing the localization matrix (if required), 
    and running a single iteration of the ensemble update equations
}
