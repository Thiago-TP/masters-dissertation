\debug{%
The job of a methods chapter is:
\begin{itemize}
    \item To summarise, explain and recount how you answered your research questions and to explain how this relates to the methods used by other scholars in similar contexts and similar studies
    \item To discuss - in detail - the techniques you used to collect the data used to answer your research questions
    \item To discuss why the techniques are relevant to the study's aims and objectives
    \item To explain how you used them
\end{itemize}
}

\debug{%
Your reader should be able to answer the following questions when they're done reading it:
\begin{itemize}
    \item What did you do to achieve the research aims?
    \item Why did you choose this particular approach over others?
    \item How does it relate to your epistemological and ontological positions?
    \item What tools did you use to collect data and why? What are the implications?
    \item When did you collect data, and from whom?
    \item What tools have you used to analyse the data and why? What are the implications? Are there ethical considerations to take into account?
\end{itemize}
}
